# Manual withdrawal payouts — runbook

Until automated payouts (Cashfree) are live, creator withdrawals are paid by the
accounts team from an Excel sheet generated by the admin portal. This is the
routine for whoever runs it.

## How a withdrawal moves

| Status shown in admin | Meaning | Creator sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Requested** (`pending`) | Creator asked to withdraw. Wallet already debited. Not yet on a sheet. | Requested |
| **Sent to accounts** (`processing`) | Included in a downloaded payment sheet. Waiting to be paid. | Processing |
| **Paid** (`completed`) | Accounts paid it; UTR recorded. | Paid + reference |
| **Failed** (`failed`) | Could not be paid. Full amount (fee included) returned to the creator's wallet. | Failed — refunded + reason |

Rules enforced by the system (not by people): lifetime earnings of ₹1,500 unlock
withdrawals; only the fixed amounts offered in the app can be requested; one
withdrawal per creator per day (IST); one unpaid withdrawal at a time. The fee
comes from `WITHDRAWAL_FEE_BPS` (default 5%) and is deducted from the amount.
Payment is promised within **7 days**.

## Daily routine

Needs the **Payouts** permission (View to look, Manage to download and record).

1. **Admin → Payouts.** The *Requested* card shows how many new requests there are.
2. **Download payment sheet.** Confirm. The requests move to *Sent to accounts* and
   an `.xlsx` downloads. Do this once per batch — rows are never exported twice.
   Lost the file? Open the row's **Sheet** button to download that batch again.
3. **Hand the file to accounts directly.** It contains full bank account numbers,
   IFSC and PAN. Do not email it or post it in chat. Accounts deletes it once the
   batch is paid.
4. **Accounts pays** each row the *Amount to pay* (already net of the fee), using
   the *Withdrawal ID* as the payment reference. UPI rows may need to be paid one
   at a time depending on the bank's bulk-upload support.
5. **Record the result**, either way:
   - **One by one:** Mark paid (enter the bank UTR) or Mark failed (enter the reason).
   - **In bulk:** accounts fills the *Paid? (Y/N)*, *UTR / reference* and *Remarks*
     columns of the same sheet and sends it back; use **Upload returned sheet**.
     Rows left blank are skipped and stay *Sent to accounts*. Any row with a
     problem is listed with the reason; fix it and upload again — rows already
     recorded are not applied twice.
6. **End of day:** the total of rows marked Paid should equal what accounts
   actually transferred. Anything still *Sent to accounts* after 7 days needs chasing.

## Things that can go wrong

- **"Paid but no UTR" / "Not paid but no remarks" in an upload:** the row is
  rejected until filled in. A failed payment must say why — the creator sees it.
- **Marking failed refunds immediately and only once.** A withdrawal that was
  already paid cannot be marked failed (and vice versa). If a transfer bounces
  *after* it was marked paid, there is no undo in the portal — raise it with
  engineering rather than adjusting balances by hand.
- **A row shows `UNREADABLE - do not pay` as the account:** the stored bank
  details could not be decrypted. Do not pay it; tell engineering. (Usually means
  `PAYOUT_ACCOUNT_ENCRYPTION_KEY` / `JWT_SECRET` changed since the creator saved
  their details.)
- **Creators editing their bank details** after requesting do not change an open
  withdrawal: it pays to the details frozen at the time of the request.

## Not covered yet

- **TDS.** Nothing deducts tax from withdrawals. Decide this with your accountant
  before the first real payout. The sheet includes the PAN for bank payouts.
- **Verification of bank / UPI details.** Details are format-checked only; nothing
  confirms the account exists or matches the holder's name. Accounts should treat
  a first payment to a new account with care.

## Deploy notes

- Run `pnpm prisma:migrate:deploy` for migration `20261008120000_manual_withdrawal_fulfilment`
  (adds the `payouts` admin section, the `withdrawal_refund` ledger type, the
  withdrawal batch table and new withdrawal columns).
- Grant the **Payouts** section to the roles that should see it (Roles & Access).
  Roles without it see nothing; Super Admins always have it.
- Check `WITHDRAWAL_FEE_BPS` in production. `.env.example` shows `150` (1.5%) but
  the code default is `500` (5%); the apps now read the fee from the server.
